WaterHeaterCue Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-10

English

WaterHeaterCue stores task names, categories, locations, dates, cycles, reminder lead times, notes, completed counts, missed counts, and recent activity on your device and in the App Group container used by widgets.

Notification permission is optional and used for local maintenance reminders. Apple Reminders access is optional and used only after you choose to sync dated tasks.

WaterHeaterCue does not collect data or include third-party advertising or tracking SDKs. It may recommend another app from the same developer; the App Store opens only after you tap. WaterHeaterCue does not create accounts, use cloud sync, control equipment remotely, or diagnose gas, electrical, carbon monoxide, rupture, or leak causes.

通知权限是可选的，只用于本地维护提醒。Apple 提醒事项权限也是可选的，只在你选择同步带日期任务后使用。

WaterHeaterCue 不收集数据，也不包含第三方广告或追踪 SDK。它可能推荐同一开发者的另一款 App；只有你主动点击后才会打开 App Store。 WaterHeaterCue 不创建账号、不使用云同步、不远程控制设备，也不诊断燃气、电气、一氧化碳、爆裂或漏水原因。
