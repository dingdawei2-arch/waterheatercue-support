WaterHeaterCue Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-10

English

WaterHeaterCue stores task names, categories, locations, dates, cycles, reminder lead times, notes, completed counts, missed counts, and recent activity on your device and in the App Group container used by widgets.

Notification permission is optional and used for local maintenance reminders. Apple Reminders access is optional and used only after you choose to sync dated tasks.

Free users may see clearly labeled Google AdMob native ads. Plus subscribers stay ad-free. Depending on your ATT choice and device settings, Google may process ad identifiers, device information, and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads. WaterHeaterCue does not create accounts, use cloud sync, control equipment remotely, or diagnose gas, electrical, carbon monoxide, rupture, or leak causes.

通知权限是可选的，只用于本地维护提醒。Apple 提醒事项权限也是可选的，只在你选择同步带日期任务后使用。

免费用户可能看到明确标注的 Google AdMob 原生广告。Plus 订阅用户无广告。根据你的 ATT 选择和设备设置，Google 可能处理广告标识符、设备信息和广告互动数据以投放和衡量广告。WaterHeaterCue 不创建账号、不使用云同步、不远程控制设备，也不诊断燃气、电气、一氧化碳、爆裂或漏水原因。
