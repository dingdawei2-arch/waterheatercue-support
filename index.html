WaterHeaterCue Support

WaterHeaterCue is a local-first reminder and record app for water heater maintenance tasks.

English

Create tasks for Tank Flush, Tankless Descale, Anode Rod Check, Leak Pan & Drain Check, Professional Service, and T&P / Relief Valve Professional Check. Keep dates, cycles, notes, completed counts, and missed counts on this device. Local notifications and widgets show the next task.

For T&P / Relief Valve entries, follow the manufacturer's instructions or contact a qualified professional. The app does not provide testing or repair steps.

For support, email dingdawei2@icloud.com with your device model, iOS version, and the issue.
